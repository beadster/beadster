# Privacy Policy

**Beadster macOS App**

effective: October 2025

## what we collect

**nothing.**

beadster is a local-only app. everything stays on your Mac.

## where your data lives

- all issue data stored in `.beads` folders inside your projects
- no cloud sync in this version
- no data sent to our servers
- no analytics
- no tracking
- no telemetry

## your data, your control

- you own all your issue data
- stored entirely on your Mac
- deleted when you remove projects or delete the app
- no backups on our servers because we never see your data

## permissions

the app requests permission to:
- access folders you explicitly select (macOS file picker)
- read and write `.beads` folders in those projects

that's it. nothing else.

## no account required

- no sign up
- no email
- no password
- no user profile
- 100% offline

## future versions

future versions may offer optional cloud sync. if we do:
- it will be opt-in only
- this policy will be updated
- you'll be notified before any data leaves your Mac

## changes

we may update this policy. major changes will be posted on our website.
